# M3 step 4: new message

Starting a conversation from the watch, as decided for M3 (29 October 2026): favourites first, then recently heard contacts, then channels.

## What it does

- **Home › New message** lists, in three parts:
  - **Favourites:** companions (people's radios) marked as favourites on the radio, by name.
  - **Recently heard:** the 20 other companions heard most recently, each with when ("Heard 4 min ago", by the time in their advert, so by their clock).
  - **Channels:** every channel on the radio, in slot order.
  Repeaters, room servers and sensors are not listed: they are not people to message.
- **Tapping one opens its conversation**, with any history and **Reply** under it. A new one says "No messages yet"; the first reply starts it, and it appears on Home.
- **A direct conversation whose contact is not a favourite** says so under Reply: the radio's contact list is full and drops contacts it has not heard from lately (found in step 1, checked overnight 29 to 30 October: favourites held, others came and went). Mark the contact a favourite in the phone app to keep it. If the radio no longer lists the contact at all, it says that messages cannot go either way until the contact advertises again.

Contacts come from the watch's copy of the radio's list, read in full each hour in Check-in and at every Live connection, so a favourite marked in the phone app appears after the next full read (or Settings › Read channels and contacts again).

## Testing on the watch

1. **The list.** Home › New message: favourites first (the companions among the five the log listed overnight), then recently heard companions with times, then the channels.
2. **A new conversation.** Pick a recently heard companion you have not messaged: an empty conversation with Reply. Send a quick reply: it appears on Home, and ends as Delivered if they are in reach.
3. **The warning.** That conversation says the contact is not a favourite; a favourite's does not.
4. **A favourite added.** Mark that contact a favourite in the phone app, then Settings › Read channels and contacts again: it moves to Favourites, and the warning goes.
5. **A channel.** Pick a channel: its conversation opens, as from Home.
